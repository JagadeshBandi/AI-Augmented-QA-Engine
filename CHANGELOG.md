## Version History Philosophy

### Semantic Versioning
- **Major (X.0.0)**: Breaking changes, major features
- **Minor (X.Y.0)**: New features, improvements
- **Patch (X.Y.Z)**: Bug fixes, documentation

### Release Cadence
- **Major Releases**: Every 6 months with significant AI advancements
- **Minor Releases**: Monthly with feature additions and improvements
